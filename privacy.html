Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

BPR Engineering Lab ("we", "us") provides AI-focused training for transportation professionals. This policy explains what information we collect through bprengineeringlab.com and our courses, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect Website analytics & cookies How we use information Who we share it with How long we keep it Your choices Contact us

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiries and reservations. When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email, phone number (if given), the courses you're interested in, and your message.
  • Course registration. When you register for a course, we keep your name, email, phone number, company, the courses you take, dates, attendance, PDH hours earned and your certificate ID.
  • Calls, texts and WhatsApp. If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we receive your number and whatever you choose to send.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, our analytics tools collect information about your visit. See the next section.

Website analytics & cookies

We use two analytics services to understand how people find and use the site, so we can improve it:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) records the pages you view, how long you stay, the site or search that referred you, your approximate location (city or region, based on your IP address), and your device and browser type.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) records how visitors interact with pages, such as clicks, scrolling and page views, and produces heatmaps and session replays. Clarity masks text typed into form fields, so we cannot see what you type in a replay.

Both services use cookies and similar technologies. We see this information in aggregate or as anonymous visits. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not use advertising or retargeting cookies.

How we use information

  • To answer your questions and hold course reservations
  • To register you, deliver courses and share course materials
  • To issue certificates of completion and record PDH hours
  • To let you look up your records and download your certificates on our Certificates page
  • To verify your PDH hours if a licensing board audits you
  • To improve the website and our courses

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and courses, and only for that purpose:

  • Formspree delivers contact form messages to us.
  • Google stores participant records (Google Workspace), hosts course materials and certificates (Google Drive), and provides Google Analytics.
  • Microsoft provides Clarity analytics.
  • GitHub hosts the website.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to confirm your attendance and PDH hours to a licensing board when you ask us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up. We keep participant records, attendance and certificate information for several years, because state licensing boards can audit PDH claims for multiple renewal cycles. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, though we may keep a basic record of completed courses so we can still verify your certificates.

Security

Participant records are kept in a private system that only we can access. The Certificates page only returns records when both your email and a valid certificate ID match. Certificate and course material links work for anyone who has the link, so please don't share them publicly.

Your choices

  • Cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Google Analytics. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on.
  • Your records. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

Learn more in the Google privacy policy and the Microsoft privacy statement.

Children

Our courses are for working professionals. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, call or text +1 (512) 686-8093, or use the contact form.
