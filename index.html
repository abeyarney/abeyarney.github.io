Why this matters now

AI is changing how traffic studies get done. Be the engineer who knows how.

Firms and agencies are under pressure to deliver more studies, faster, without letting quality slip. The engineers who can put AI to work, safely and correctly, are the ones who finish first, catch problems earlier and produce work that stands out.

This isn't generic AI training. Every course is built on workflows proven on real transportation projects, where AI tools were used to increase speed, improve accuracy and streamline QC. You'll learn exactly where AI helps, where it doesn't, and how to check its work like an engineer.

Built for

  • Traffic and transportation engineers who want to deliver studies faster without cutting corners.
  • Planners and analysts who spend too much time on data cleanup, memos and slides.
  • Project managers and QC reviewers who want more consistent, reliable reviews.
  • Agency staff reviewing consultant work and modernizing internal processes.
  • Students and early-career engineers who want an edge that most of their peers don't have yet.

The AI-enhanced workflow

AI at every stage of the study. Engineering judgment at every decision.

Together, the courses cover the full life of a traffic study, the same way AI is used on live projects.

  1. 01

    Data

    Clean and organize traffic counts, balance volumes and apply growth rates much faster.

  2. 02

    Analysis

    Set up Synchro, HCS and VISSIM inputs faster, automate repetitive steps and interpret delay, queue and LOS results.

  3. 03

    QC

    Run structured AI-assisted checks that catch input errors and inconsistencies before a reviewer does.

  4. 04

    Reporting

    Draft clear technical memos and reports, and summarize manuals and guidance with NotebookLM.

  5. 05

    Presentation

    Turn results into client-ready slides and plain-language summaries for stakeholders and the public.

AI assists. Engineers decide.

Every course covers verification, data confidentiality and professional responsibility. You'll know how to check AI output against the HCM and agency standards so your work stays defensible and your stamp stays protected.
